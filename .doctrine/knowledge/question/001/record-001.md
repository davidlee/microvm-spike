needs the sideband half it was never briefed with — two answers:

1. **A scoped additive verb.** `--only-absent`, same validation, same `code-oid`,

**The second is cleaner; the first is what the day wanted.**

window rather than serving a capsule already inside it** — which is precisely the
case item 45 was written about. So the evidence accumulated so far is evidence
about a different question.

What would settle it: a capsule genuinely inside the window, and a note on
whether the operator reached for the verb or restarted. Related to `IDE-001` —
the sideband arc is what hand-driving turned out to need, and the requirements
